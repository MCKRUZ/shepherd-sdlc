## Goal
A named person can settle what `pull` found on the board, and see the mirror from the desktop app, without either side guessing. `ado_sync.py reconcile <ref> --field F --take ado|sdlc|ack|not-drift --by <human>` records a disposition on one drift row through the design §8 state machine, and a `--take ado` on a board-owned field spawns the verb that owns that field (`spec_transition.py`, `sprint.py`, `track_decisions.py`) with that verb's own argv, never a rewrite of the spec by the mirror; findings become `BugCategory` work items through a new `SOURCES["finding"]` loader; `pull --propose-specs` proposes a spec for a board item nobody mapped. Shepherd, the desktop app, gains a work-item chip on the spec card, an apply dialog, a discussion block with a comment composer, and two Today rows, every one fed by `ado_sync.py status --json` / `show --json` through a closed argv table, with `--by` the signed-in person and nothing on a timer.

## Why
Fold B3 made the mirror write; a board that changes under the repository is now a daily fact, and without a way to settle it every drift row stays open forever, so the debt count the hook prints means nothing. The settling must not become a second write path: a developer change on an in-flight spec is a new hand-off (`handoff.py`'s rule), a state change is `spec_transition.py`'s, a sprint change is `sprint.py`'s, so `reconcile` relays to the owning verb and records its exit instead of editing anything itself. The desktop app is where most people will meet the mirror, and it is the privileged half of the product (the Electron main process spawns plugin scripts as the signed-in person), so its delta is held to the existing pattern exactly: a closed request type from the renderer, a pure golden-tested argv table in which a comment's text is one `--message=<text>` element, the actor resolved in main and appended last, one spawn per verb, and no read of `.sdlc/ado/**` from the renderer.

## Risk Tier
**Tier:** HIGH
**Why this tier:** this Fold changes `studio/electron/**`, a security-gated path (`.github/workflows/security.yml`): the Electron main process is the privileged half of the desktop app, and it now spawns plugin verbs that write to an external board as the signed-in person. A request the renderer could shape into extra argv, a comment text that split into flags, a `--by` taken from the renderer instead of main, or a reconcile that relayed `--by` to a verb that does not take it (argparse exit 2 read as "refused") would each put words or decisions in a person's name. The guards are mechanical: a closed request type, a pure golden-tested argv table, the actor appended last by main, one spawn per verb, the per-verb `CHILD_ARGV` table, and a renderer that never reads the mirror's cache. The Pod Lead may not lower it.

## Decision List
- **Free text is one argv element.** A comment's message, a reconcile reason and an owner travel as `--message=<text>`, `--reason=<text>` and `--owner=<text>`, so a text with a quote, a newline, `$(` or a leading dash is one value to argparse; the message is sent byte for byte, never trimmed. Owner: @MCKRUZ. Answer: the `--flag=<text>` form, golden-tested.
- **Studio reads JSON, never prose.** Every Boards verb the table builds ends in `--json` before `--by`, so main reads the plugin's own document (the composer's `apply --confirm` hash comes from `comment --json`, checked against `^[0-9a-f]{12}$` before it is sent). Owner: @MCKRUZ. Answer: `--json` on all 7 verbs.
- **The take words name four dispositions.** `ado`, `sdlc`, `ack` and `not-drift` map to `TAKE_ADO`, `TAKE_SDLC`, `ACKNOWLEDGED` and `NOT_DRIFT`; a take the machine does not allow for a row's class maps to `None` and is refused, and `RECREATE`, `UNLINK` and `RELINK` have no take word in the model. Owner: @MCKRUZ. Answer: refuse, never guess.
- **The one existing-test edit is the capability pin.** `studio/test/reasons.test.ts` gains the 6 Boards ids in its exact list (its title names them); every other assertion stays as it was, and the new sentences are tested in the new `studio/test/adoVerbArgv.test.ts`. Owner: @MCKRUZ. Answer: extend the pin, nothing else.
