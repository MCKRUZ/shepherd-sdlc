Olive Field

One blended primary that sits between green and brown — an olive that reads as living earth rather than either parent. Umber and moss become its shadow and highlight, so the whole palette is one hue family with lake as the single cool note.

Palette

  • Olive — primary#54632C
  • Moss — highlight#3F6B35
  • Umber — shadow#5E452A
  • Lake — information#2F5D7C
  • Bark — text#1C1915
  • Warm sand — background#F5EFE4

Logo ideas

  • A single-stroke olive crook, lit moss on one edge and umber on the other
  • A fold arc whose gradient runs moss to umber across the discs
  • A horizon drawn as one olive band with a moss point above
