Loam

Umber brown leads, which is rare in software tooling and shares no hue with any status colour. Moss and lake read as the living things on brown earth.

Palette

  • Umber — primary#6B4A2B
  • Moss — secondary#3F6B35
  • Lake — information#2F5D7C
  • Clay — light accent#C9A27A
  • Bark — text#1C1915
  • Sand — background#F7F4EE
