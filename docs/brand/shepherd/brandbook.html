1. Name

The product is Shepherd. One word, one capital. It names two things together: the shepherd-sdlc plugin for Claude Code and its Electron desktop app. A shepherd walks the flock through a gate one at a time and keeps the fold closed; the plugin walks each spec through its gates, one spec on one branch to one pull request, and nothing passes a gate that did not earn it.

WrittenShepherd. Capital S, the rest lower-case. Never shepherd as the name, never SHEPHERD, never Shepherd™.
SpokenSHEP-erd. Two syllables, stress on the first.
Refers toThe plugin and the desktop app as one product. In code, the app's folder is still studio/, its package is sdlc-studio and the renderer bridge is window.studio; the screens, window title, packaged app name and slash-command prefix (/sprd-*) are Shepherd's.
Kept on purpose.sdlc/, the seven gates, "SDLC" as the name of the lifecycle. Brand is a surface, not a schema: what a project stores does not change its name.
Slugshepherd. ASCII, lower-case, for paths and file names only: docs/brand/shepherd/, shepherd.ico, shepherd-tile-512.png. The slug is a file name. It is not the name.

The mark is a gate with the letter S inside it. The frame is the gate: two halves, green and brown, that meet at the corners and leave a gap. The S is what passes through — and it rests on the tab, so it has been let in, not left out.

2. The mark

Mark A, the gate-S, is the mark. It is in the product: the window icon, the tile, the favicon, the Welcome hero, the opening card, the steering lockup. It is two-tone by design — the moss half acts (top and right, with the tab), the umber half holds (left and bottom) — and the two values never change between themes. There are no alternates: the owner's two earlier concepts are archived in brandkit/drafts and are not used anywhere.

The solid two-tone mark is the mark. It has one sanctioned hero treatment, Depth: the same mark with a single gradient laid over its silhouette (§2 "Mark A — Depth"). Depth lives in exactly four places — the Welcome hero, the opening card, the app icon and the steering-mode title lockup — and nowhere else.

Mark A — primary

Mark A — Depth

The hero treatment. The two-tone mark stays exactly as drawn, and one linear gradient in the mark's own units is laid over its silhouette at 30 % opacity. The gradient runs from the frame's top-left corner (4, 4) to its bottom-right corner (60, 60) — a vector perpendicular to both chamfer seams, so the two halves meet at the same stop and read as one object lit from one side rather than two tinted pieces. The stops are two steps of the moss ramp and follow the theme: on light grounds moss 300 → moss 900 (#9DC58E → #192D17), on dark grounds #9DC58E → #3F6B35 — 300 and the shared 600 — so the dark mark stays luminous rather than sinking into the surface. The overlay is judged, not derived: at 55 % the umber half read as olive and the two tones merged; at 30 % both tones survive and the mark still reads lit. Depth carries no meaning. It is allowed at 48 px and above, in the four hero places only (§4): never on a control, a chip, a status, a data body or behind text — there the solid mark stays.

3. Construction and clear space

The mark is drawn on a 64-unit grid. Four filled paths, no strokes: two frame halves that are mirror images across the main diagonal, and a two-tone S.

Tile and lockup

The tile is the mark inset, not a new drawing. A 64 square with corner radius 14 in sand (#F5EFE4); the mark at 7/8 — translate(4 4) scale(0.875), so the frame's outer edge sits 7.5 units in from every side and its chamfers stay clear of the rounded corner. tile.svg carries the frame alone, because at 16 and 32 px the S is three pixels of noise; tile-depth.svg carries the full mark with the Depth overlay, for 64 px and above. The two tiles share the inset, so the icon does not jump size between the 32 and 64 px slots.

The lockup places the mark on its own 64-unit canvas and starts the word at x 80, baseline y 46. Its gap is 20 units from the frame's right edge (60), one bar thickness plus the S's own half-width of air. The word is the outlined wordmark at font size 48: Saira's x-height of 24.5 puts the lowercase band at y 21.5–46, on the S inside the frame (y 23–44.5), so the letters and the S read at one height; the ascenders of h and d reach y 10.3, inside the frame's top edge (4), and the p's descender y 55.5, inside its bottom (60). The word ends at x 280.3 on the 320 × 64 canvas. The wordmark alone (wordmark.svg) is 201 × 64 on the same baseline, its left edge at x 0.

5. Colour

Palette "Furrow": earth colours. Moss is the accent — it acts: the mark's green half, primary buttons, focus, "now". Umber is the support — it holds: the mark's brown half, structure, warm emphasis. Lake is information: links, the steering tint, a second chart series. Surfaces are sand in light and bark in dark; ink is bark on sand and sand on bark. Four semantic colours carry meaning and do not change between themes.

One accent, semantic colours mean one thing each. Green is always signed. Amber is always a warning. Red is always an error. Sky is always running. Moss is a green too — so it is a dark olive green (6.24:1 on white) where signed is a bright emerald (3.30:1), the two never share a lightness, and signed is always paired with its word on screen. The accent never carries status; status never borrows the accent.

7. Voice

Shepherd keeps a gate. It lets one spec through at a time, says plainly what held and what did not, and never pretends the fold is closed when it is not. The writing follows.

  • Honest. "no data" is text, never a zero. A missing value says what would produce one: "no data. Run sprint.py close on a sprint to see rework."
  • The plugin's words, not the app's. READY and NOT READY. Kept, carried, dropped. "Signed off by Priya Nair." "Phase 0: Discovery." "G3 metrics failed." The desktop app shows what the plugin reports and does not rename it, round it, or draw a shape from its prose.
  • One spec at a time. One spec, one branch, one pull request. A sentence about the work names the spec, not "the sprint's changes".
  • The gate is a fact. A gate held or it did not; a check passed or it failed. No "soft fail", no "minor issues", no "mostly green".
  • Short declarative sentences. One idea per sentence. Tables where they help.
  • Named humans. Every decision names who made it. An AI is never the actor. A name that reads as one is refused.
  • Counts, never scores. Specs slated, business days waited, who holds the next action. Never velocity, story points, PR count, lines of code, or a per-person total.
  • Calm. No exclamation marks in the product. No marketing adjectives. The setup confirmation block in the plugin ends with one "!" and that is the budget.

Signature motion

The mark moves twice in the desktop app and nowhere else: once when the app first opens (the assemble), and once on the card that covers the window while a project opens (the draw-in). The draw-in is one gesture in three beats under 0.9 s — the two frame halves are traced as outlines, each in its own tone; the frame fills as the trace hands over; the S settles onto the tab from its own centre — then the mark rests; the ring beside the clock, not the mark, is what keeps saying "still working". The gesture is the brand's; the shape is not a canvas for it. Another element (a rail station, a plate) may trace-fill-settle once as it arrives. Nothing may draw the gate and S that is not the mark, and the mark never loops, pulses or spins.

8. Do and don't

Do

  • Use mark A two-tone — moss 600 and umber 700 — or in one colour: ink 1, or white. The solid two-tone mark is the mark.
  • Keep the 64-unit proportions, the gap at the seams, the S's seat on the tab, and the clear space.
  • Use the tile for the favicon and app icon — the frame alone, flat, at 16 and 32 px; the full mark in Depth from 64 px. In the app, the frame alone at 24 px and the full mark from 48 px.
  • Use Depth only at 48 px and above, only as a hero: the Welcome hero, the opening card, the steering lockup, the app icon. Always the one construction (§2): one gradient, corner to corner, over the whole silhouette at 30 %.
  • Set the name as one capitalised word in every language and every system — Shepherd. Only the drawn wordmark is lowercase.
  • Use the wordmark files — wordmark.svg, wordmark-white.svg, lockup.svg — never a retyped word. The wordmark is "shepherd" in Saira 600, lowercase, −0.01em, outlined; in running text the name is Shepherd in the UI face.
  • Pair every status colour with the plugin's word for it.

Don't

  • Do not outline the mark, add a stroke, or place it inside a second shape other than the tile.
  • Do not stretch, skew, thicken a bar, or move the S off the tab.
  • Do not swap the tones: moss is always the top-and-right half with the tab and the S's upper lobe; umber the left-and-bottom half and the lower lobe.
  • Do not place the mark on a photograph, a gradient, or a patterned ground. (Depth puts the gradient on the mark, never under it.)
  • Do not use a second gradient mechanism — no 3D mesh, no shadow, no bevel. Depth is the one treatment and has the one construction.
  • Do not use anything from brandkit/drafts or brandkit/logo: they are the road to the mark, not marks.
  • Do not animate the mark except its two signature moments in the desktop app: the first-open assemble and the opening card's draw-in (the halves are traced, the frame fills, the S settles). The gesture — trace, fill, settle — may be reused by other elements; the shape may not (§7, Signature motion).
